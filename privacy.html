Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 1, 2026

This application is for personal use with rclone and Google Drive.

Data Access

The application accesses Google Drive files and metadata as authorized by the account owner.

Data Usage

Google Drive data is used for personal file transfers, synchronization, and management.

Data Storage

rclone stores OAuth credentials locally. Files may be transferred to storage destinations configured by the user.

Data Sharing

This application is not offered to other users. Data is transferred only to destinations configured by the account owner.

Revoking Access

Access can be revoked through Google Account security settings.
